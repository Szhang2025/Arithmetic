6. The Negative Secrets ❄️

Walking Rule Simulator: (-5) × (-3) = 15

Initially, the person stands at the origin (0), facing Right (Positive).
🚶‍♂️

Interactive Discovery: Why does (-5) × (-3) = 15?

(-5) × 2 = -10
(-5) × 1 = -5 (+5)
(-5) × 0 = 0 (+5)
(-5) × (-1) = 5 (+5)
(-5) × (-2) = 10 (+5)
(-5) × (-3) = 15 (+5)

🎯 Quick Summary

  • ✅ Same Signs: Result is always Positive.
  • ✅ Different Signs: Result is always Negative.
